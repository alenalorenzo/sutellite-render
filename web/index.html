About the data

Historical calendar

The NASA FIRMS archive covers 2000–2025 across eight regions. Select a satellite to see its own observations. The harmonized series uses MODIS estimates on the VIIRS S-NPP scale through January 2012 and S-NPP observations from February 2012. The regional calibration uses the 2013–2022 overlap. Early cell counts are proportional estimates. Historical comparisons use at least three earlier years with observations for the same month and source.

Uploaded files and hotspot cells

Upload a UTF-8 CSV or Parquet. Each source and region has its own calendar and spatial summary. The comparison uses available earlier years in the same file. Detections with locations are drawn in 0.5° cells. The archive uses the dominant class from the ESA WorldCover 2021 reference in each cell. A class is available for uploaded detections in cells covered by the site’s lookup.

Contains modified Copernicus Sentinel data (2021) processed by the ESA WorldCover consortium. ESA WorldCover 2021 v200 · CC BY 4.0

Historical anomalies

The historical map uses the MODIS Terra and Aqua and VIIRS S-NPP archive in canonical_v1.1. Earlier MODIS counts are estimated on the VIIRS S-NPP scale using the 2013–2022 overlap. Those estimates use regional ratios, so the earlier points are approximate at grid-cell level. A hotspot is a satellite detection.

Each 0.1° cell is compared with the same calendar month in earlier years with usable detections. A point is marked when the low estimate exceeds the highest earlier upper estimate, using at least three earlier years. VIIRS counts are used directly. Months with no records have unknown coverage. January 2012 uses MODIS because VIIRS S-NPP started partway through the month.

2024–2025 AI results

The original Isolation Forest results use the 2013–2023 VIIRS S-NPP history. An alert also exceeds its historical maximum.

Burned area and land cover

Each point marks a grid cell center. In the 2024–2025 AI results, the MODIS category indicates whether burned area was mapped within the cell for that month. ESA WorldCover 2021 provides the land cover class. The satellite basemap shows a reference view of the terrain.
