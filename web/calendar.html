How reliable is the estimated period?

  • Before 2012, MODIS counts are scaled by a regional S-NPP/MODIS ratio per calendar month, estimated on 2013–2022. Day and night are pooled.
  • Reference platform is S-NPP only, so the series does not jump when NOAA-20/21 come online.
  • Validation: leave-one-year-out on 2013–2022, predicting regional monthly S-NPP totals. Smaller areas will have larger errors.
  • No observation-opportunity correction and no event matching. NRT records (after Jul 2026) are not merged.
